Management Skills Cigna Project

Access Project Here: Management Skills Cigna Project

This project was a team project I worked on during my Management Skills Class at Rutgers, where I was given the opportunity to interview different levels of managers at Cigna to understand how their organization is structured, their roles, and to discover possible gaps in management style based on different concepts we learned in class. We were able to identify two management-type concerns based on our interviews and were able to come up with two recommendations on how these issues could be resolved. It was a great experience to not only speak to business professionals but use what we learned during our class and implement it into real business situations.

Frozen Garden Marketing Campaign

Access Project Here: Frozen Garden Marketing Campaign

This team project was a digital marketing campaign worked on during my marketing class at RBS where we entered a digital marketing competition through coming up with a campaign for a real client. The client, Frozen Garden, gave us a brief and the goals of their campaign, and my team and I had to come up with a digital marketing campaign to identify a new target audience and propose a marketing plan to reach this new audience. We conducted market research and received responses from different target audiences to identify the strongest target population, and were able to tailor a plan to specifically reach that target population. Although we weren't selected, it was a great experience to work on a brief from a real client and get experience in conducting real world market research as well as creating our own marketing campaign.
